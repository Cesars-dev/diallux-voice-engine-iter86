## What if the AI doesn't know an answer

The agent never invents answers. When a question is outside what it can confirm, it adapts tone, stays honest, and hands off — it can warm-transfer to a human agent when a call needs a person, so nothing gets improvised and nothing gets lost.

---

## AI vs a real person doing the job

The agent isn't trying to replace people — it handles the calls humans can't get to: every call answered instantly 24/7, hundreds of simultaneous calls, appointment booking, and consistent answers on repeat questions. Anything that needs a person's judgment gets warm-transferred to a human. Routine coverage goes to the AI, judgment calls go to people.
