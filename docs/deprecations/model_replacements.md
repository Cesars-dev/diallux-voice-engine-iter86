# Model Replacements

## ElevenLabs Turbo → Flash (07/12/2026)

**Source:** https://docs.retellai.com/deprecation-notice/2026/07-12_elevenlabs_turbo_models

ElevenLabs Turbo voice models are being deprecated and automatically migrated to their Flash counterparts on 7/12/2026. Flash delivers the same voice quality at lower latency. No action required.

## Claude Sonnet & Gemini Flash (05/25/2026)

**Source:** https://docs.retellai.com/deprecation-notice/2026/05-25_model_replacements

Claude and Gemini models are being deprecated and automatically migrated to newer versions on 5/25/2026. No action required.

## OpenAI Realtime & Cartesia Sonic (04/03/2026)

**Source:** https://docs.retellai.com/deprecation-notice/2026/04-03_model_replacements

OpenAI Realtime and Cartesia Sonic models are being deprecated and replaced with newer versions. After 4/3/2026, the deprecated models will no longer be available. Automatic migration.
